Chemistry

  • ▶ Atomic Structure

    Definition

    Atomic structure is the study of atoms, their subatomic particles, and how electrons are arranged around the nucleus.

    Important Facts

    • Atoms contain protons, neutrons and electrons.
    • Electron arrangement determines chemical properties.
    • Atomic number = number of protons.
    • ▶ Periodic Table

      Definition

      The periodic table organizes elements according to their atomic numbers and properties.

      Important Facts

      Important Facts

      • 118 known elements.
      • Elements in the same group have similar properties.
      • Periods represent electron shells.
      • ▶ Inorganic Chemistry

        Studies metals, minerals, salts and non-carbon compounds.

        • Includes acids, bases and salts.
        • Important in industries.
      • ▶ Organic Chemistry

        Studies carbon compounds.

        • Basis of life.
        • Includes hydrocarbons and polymers.
      • ▶ Coordination Chemistry

        Studies metal complexes and ligands.

        • Important in biology and medicine.
  • ▶ Chemical Bonding

    Chemical bonding explains how atoms combine.

    • Ionic bonds
    • Covalent bonds
    • Metallic bonds
  • ▶ States of Matter

    Matter exists as solid, liquid, gas and plasma.

  • ▶ Chemical Reactions

    Chemical reactions transform substances into new products.

    • ▶ Thermodynamics

      Studies energy changes in reactions.

    • ▶ Kinetics

      Studies reaction rates.

    • ▶ Equilibrium

      Studies reversible reactions.

  • ▶ Acids & Bases

    Acids donate H+ ions and bases accept them.

  • ▶ Electrochemistry

    Studies chemical reactions involving electricity.

  • ▶ Analytical Chemistry

    Identifies and measures chemical substances.
