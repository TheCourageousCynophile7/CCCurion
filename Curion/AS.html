Atomic structure:

Atoms are important buiding blocks of matter. They are made up of a dense, central nucleus containing positively charged protons and neutral neutrons. The neucleas holds 99.9% of the atom's mass. Surrounding the neucleas in a vast cloud of tiny, negatively charged electrons.

Subatomic Particle Properties:

  • Protons: The positively charged particles located in the dense center of the atom. The number of protons determines the element's identity and atomic number. positive charge(+1)
  • Neutrons:Particles with no electrical charge. They are situated in the nucleus and prevent the positively charged protons from repelling each other. Neutral charge(0)
  • Electrons: Tiny negatively charged particles that orbit the nucleus at very high speeds. They are about 2,000 times lighter than protons and are responsible for chemical bonds and interaction. Negative charge(-1)

Atomic models over time:

  • Dalton model(1803): Proposed atoms were invisible, solid spheres.
  • Thomson model(1897): Discovered the electron, proposing a "plum pudding" model where electrons floated in a positive sphere.
  • Rutherford Model(1911): Fired alpha particles at gold foil, discovering that the atom has a tiny, dense and positive core(the nucleus).
  • Bohr model(1913):Suggested electrons orbit the nucleus in specific, fixed circular paths or energy levels(shells)
  • Quantum mechanical Model(Modern): Refines Bohr's model by describing electrons as wave-like entities that exist in probability clouds called orbitals rather than precise, fixed tracks.
