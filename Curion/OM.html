Our mission: 

 Our mission is to promote understanding among students instead of promoting rote-learning.
 We believe that studying should actually be a fun thing to do instead of  a tedious task.
 We aim to make science fun, understandable, and break complex topics into small-bite-
sized information that is linked to each other.
 We also make studying and science more interesting by showcasing interesting articles about the wonders of science,
 and provide hand-drawn mind maps for better understanding.
